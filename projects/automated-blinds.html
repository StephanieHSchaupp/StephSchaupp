Integrated PCB & Enclosure for Automated Blinds

A compact, low-cost controller board for motorized horizontal blinds. It pairs an ESP32 with a brushed DC motor driver and fits a custom 3D-printed enclosure.

01Challenge

Build a cost-effective, compact, and reliable PCB for an automated blind controller that integrates an ESP32 and a brushed DC motor driver.

02Approach

I did schematic capture and layout in KiCad, then designed the enclosure in SolidWorks to fit the board’s outline and connectors so the PCB and printed shell go together cleanly. I’m prototyping and verifying functionality on the bench.

03Status

In progress. Results will be added once testing is complete.
