Aerodynamic & Structural Optimization of a HAWT

Designed a small horizontal-axis wind turbine for maximum power at a 25 mph design wind speed while keeping weight and deflection low.

aerodynamicsHAWT designFEAstructural analysisSolidWorksCAD
Project report (PDF)

01Challenge

Maximize power output at a 25 mph target wind speed while minimizing weight and deflection.

02Approach

I chose the SG 6043 airfoil, a three-blade rotor, and a 43° twist angle to balance lift-to-drag ratio against structural integrity. I then ran finite element analysis in SolidWorks on the tower and blades to find critical stress points and improve stiffness.

03Result

The final design produced 12.63 W with a stiffness of 17.7 N/mm.
