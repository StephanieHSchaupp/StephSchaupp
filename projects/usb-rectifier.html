Power Quality of a Full-Bridge Rectifier for USB Charging

Designed and simulated a full-bridge rectifier front end for a USB charger, trading off component size against power quality.

PLECSrectifiersTHDpower factorripplecomponent sizing
Report (PDF)PLECS model

01Challenge

Design a rectifier that meets USB power specifications while keeping harmonic distortion and output ripple in check.

02Approach

I built a PLECS simulation to analyze steady-state behavior, focusing on power factor, total harmonic distortion, and output ripple voltage. I chose components to keep the design small while still meeting power-quality targets.

03Result

The final design reached a power factor of 0.9 and THD of 50%, and met the voltage and ripple constraints.
