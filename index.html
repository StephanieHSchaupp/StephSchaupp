About

I’m an Energy Engineering student at UC Berkeley with a concentration in Electrical Engineering, graduating in May 2027. I care about sustainable technology and electric mobility, and I like working at the point where circuit design meets real hardware.

My work spans converter design, PCB layout and SI/PI, DFM/DFT, and test automation. What I enjoy most is bring-up: building a board one subsystem at a time and using the scope, thermal camera, and e-load to figure out why it isn’t matching the model yet.
