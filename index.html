Projects

Aerodynamic and Structural Optimization of a HAWT

Challenge: Designed a horizontal-axis wind turbine to maximize power output for a target wind speed of 25 mph while minimizing weight and deflection.

Action: Selected the SG 6043 airfoil, 3-blade geometry, and twist angle of 43 degrees, balancing the lift-to-drag ratio with structural integrity. Performed Finite Element Analysis in Solidworks to refine the tower and blade design, identifying critical stress points and ensuring stiffness.

Result: The final design achieved an optimized power of 12.63 W with a stiffness of 17.7 N/mm.

Skills: Aerodynamics, HAWT Design, FEA, Structural Analysis, CAD, Solidworks

Thermodynamic Analysis of a Two-Stage Cascade Refrigeration Cycle

Challenge: Analyze a two-stage cascade refrigeration system utilizing NH3 and CO2 refrigerants to identify the optimal inter-stage saturation temperature for maximum Coefficient of Performance.

Action: Developed a computational model in a Jupyter Notebook to analyze the thermodynamic properties and energy balance across the system, varying the evaporation and condensation temperatures.

Result: The analysis determined the optimal intermediate temperature to be 28.07 °C, yielding a maximum COP of 4.95 and a projected cooling capacity of 225.08 kW.

Skills: Thermodynamics, Refrigeration Cycles, Python, Data Analysis, Energy Balance

Power Quality Analysis of a Full-Bridge Rectifier for USB Charging

Challenge: Design and analyze a rectifier circuit to meet USB power specifications while minimizing harmonic distortion and output ripple.

Action: Created a comprehensive simulation setup in PLECS to analyze steady-state qualities, specifically focusing on Power Factor, Total Harmonic Distortion, and ripple voltage. Components were selected to minimize size requirements while maintaining power quality.

Result: The final design achieved a THD of 50% and a power factor of 0.9, successfully demonstrating compliance with voltage and ripple constraints.

Skills: Power Electronics, PLECS Simulation, Rectifier Circuits, THD Analysis, Power Factor Correction, Circuit Design
